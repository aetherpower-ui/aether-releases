# Release licensing information

This repository is a distribution catalogue for products and components with different licences. There is no single repository-wide software licence that replaces the terms of every attached release file. This document records where those terms are found; it does not relicense the release assets.

## Nexus

The RC11 source and application packages include `LICENSE.txt`, which identifies the current Nexus distribution as an evaluation build. An unchanged copy of that existing notice is provided at [licenses/NEXUS-RC11.txt](licenses/NEXUS-RC11.txt). Included open-source components retain their separate licence files and notices.

The current evaluation notice is not a final commercial end-user licence. Seller, support and final commercial terms remain to be completed before paid distribution is enabled.

## Aether Apps and Enhance

The 0.3.0 source bundle includes `licenses/` directories, component notices and Enhance engine licence files. Those notices apply to their respective components. Do not interpret a third-party MIT, BSD, Apache or GPL notice as a blanket licence for all original Aether application code and artwork.

Final original-code licensing terms for the complete app suite are not supplied by this catalogue. Consult the notices accompanying the exact asset and the [published product-scope information](https://aether-project.net/en/licences). Do not assume that the existence of a source archive grants permissions that its applicable licence does not state.

## Aether OS and third-party packages

Debian and other bundled packages retain their own licences, copyrights and source obligations. Installed package notices are available under `/usr/share/doc/<package>/copyright`; package-source links and currently published Aether source archives are listed in the [project source index](https://aether-project.net/en/project#source).

Aether feature-licence plans do not replace or restrict rights granted by those component licences. This catalogue is not a certification that every distribution obligation has been independently reviewed.

## Questions

For the terms of a particular release file, identify its exact filename and version when using the [project contact page](https://aether-project.net/en/help). No licence for unrelated third-party games, services or AI providers is included by this catalogue.
